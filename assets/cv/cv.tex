\section{Selected Engineering Projects}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,nosep,itemsep=9pt]

  \item \textbf{Fully-Autonomous UAV Intelligent Inspection System for High-Speed Railway}
  \begin{description}[itemsep=3pt,parsep=0pt]
    \item[\textbf{Background \& Objective:}]Owing to the tight maintenance-window constraints and blind spots in inspection coverage of high-speed railway infrastructure, built the domestically- and internationally-first fully-autonomous UAV intelligent inspection system for high-speed railways and scaled its application.
    \item[\textbf{Core Methodology:}]Designed a cloud-edge collaborative architecture: the drone-on-edge autonomously captures multimodal data of rail-transit facilities, while the cloud performs point-cloud processing and intelligent analysis over multiple asset classes, achieving integrated inspection management and control spanning command/dispatch and data backhaul.
    \item[\textbf{Outcome \& Benefit:}]Scaled to about 100 km of line on the world's busiest Beijing--Shanghai railway; field-deployed 6 inspection UAVs and 4 automated drone nests; inspection efficiency is improved more than 10$\times$ over manual work, realizing routine automated inspection.
  \end{description}

  \item \textbf{Autonomous UAV Inspection of Qinghai--Tibet Railway Bridges \& Rockfall Hazards}
  \begin{description}[itemsep=3pt,parsep=0pt]
    \item[\textbf{Background \& Objective:}]Aimed at inspection difficulties of key bridge sections and rockfall hazards on hillsides around tunnel portals under extreme high altitude, low atmospheric pressure and strong wind, explored ``Smart Sky Road'' autonomous UAV inspection.
    \item[\textbf{Core Methodology:}]Fused point-cloud segmentation, intelligent flight routing and generative enhancement of defects to build an edge--cloud co-inspection recognition model for bridges and tunnels; via multi-temporal point-cloud registration, hillside feature extraction and change analysis, realized three-dimensional quantitative assessment of surrounding hillsides.
    \item[\textbf{Outcome \& Benefit:}]Performed defect detection and rockfall/deformation inspection for key zones of long plateau bridges at an average altitude above 3000 m; formed an operationally applicable technical scheme for plateau-railway UAV inspection, supporting the long-term safe operation of the Qinghai--Tibet railway.
  \end{description}

  \item \textbf{Full-Coverage UAV Inspection \& Coating-Defect Evaluation for Railway Steel Truss Bridge}
  \begin{description}[itemsep=3pt,parsep=0pt]
    \item[\textbf{Background \& Objective:}]For large-span railway steel truss girder bridges where manual full-coverage of coating disease is difficult under narrow/complex under-bridge space and GNSS-denied conditions, carried out automated inspection and defect evaluation.
    \item[\textbf{Core Methodology:}]Designed obstacle-avoidance inspection routes covering the deck, sides and the complex under-bridge zone (safe flight achieved through vision-based obstacle-space modelling together with obstacle-avoidance path search and execution); built an end-to-end evaluation workflow of ``coating-defect recognition $\to$ quantitative grading $\to$ 3-D spatial tracing''.
    \item[\textbf{Outcome \& Benefit:}]Supports long-term disease comparison and structural-safety assessment of railway steel truss bridge coating maintenance, and accumulates a reusable operation specification for UAV inspection of long-span steel bridges.
  \end{description}

\end{enumerate}
